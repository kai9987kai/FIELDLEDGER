Scientific Accountability & Version 1 Boundaries

  • Observation Rate ≠ True Abundance: Model EFFORT_RATE_V1 measures detections per observer-hour without imperfect-detection probability correction (reserved for v2 occupancy modelling).
  • Missing ≠ Zero: Absent species rows remain missingness and are never imputed as zero counts; explicit zeros require NOT_DETECTED with count = 0.
  • Association ≠ Causal Proof: BACI estimates require defensible control comparability, parallel-trends assumptions, and absence of unmeasured differential confounding.
  • Small-Sample Restraint: 95% site-cluster bootstrap intervals are intentionally withheld when fewer than 5 complete independent sites exist in either group; p-values are never manufactured.
