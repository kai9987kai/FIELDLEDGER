## 7. Transparent Calculation Methodology

1. **Effort Standardisation:** `Rate = Count * 60 / Survey_Minutes` (`detections_per_hour`), computed in COBOL fixed-point packed decimal (`COMP-3`, `S9(11)V9(6) ROUNDED`).
2. **Site-Period Aggregation:** For each `(species_code, method)` stratum and site, mean PRE and POST rates are averaged strictly over explicit observations (`DETECTED` and explicit `NOT_DETECTED` zero records). Missing rows are never imputed as zero.
3. **Same-Day Intervention Exclusion:** Surveys occurring on `survey_date = reference_date` are classified as `TRANSITION` and excluded from primary PRE/POST means.
4. **Equal-Site-Weighted BACI Estimator:** Each complete site contributes one `Site Change = POST mean - PRE mean`. Group changes are unweighted means across complete sites so survey frequency does not dominate the estimate: `BACI = Impact Change - Control Change`.
5. **Site-Cluster Bootstrap Uncertainty:** Deterministic Park-Miller LCG (`a = 16807`, `m = 2147483647`) resampling complete independent sites with replacement. Nominal 95% percentile intervals (2.5th to 97.5th order-statistic interpolation) are reported ONLY when at least 5 complete independent sites exist in both Impact and Control groups. P-values are never manufactured.

## 8. Scientific Limitations

- **Observation Rates vs. True Abundance:** Version 1 (`EFFORT_RATE_V1`) quantifies effort-standardised detection rates. It does NOT estimate true ecological abundance and does NOT correct for imperfect species detection probability (reserved for the v2 repeated-visit occupancy/detection extension interface).
- **Causal Interpretation:** A BACI estimate is an observational difference-in-differences quantity, NOT proof of causation. Causal attribution requires defensible control-site comparability, parallel-trends assumptions, and absence of unmeasured differential confounding.
- **Small-Sample Uncertainty:** With fewer than 5 complete independent sites per group, inferential confidence intervals are intentionally withheld rather than manufactured.
- **Study-Design Bias:** Neither bootstrap intervals nor sensitivity ranges remove bias introduced by unrepresentative sites or non-comparable survey protocols.
